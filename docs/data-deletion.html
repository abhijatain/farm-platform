Request deletion of your account information

Email [PUBLIC PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL] with subject Farm Platform Data Deletion Request. Tell us that you wish to delete your Farm Platform data and identify the account using the phone number linked to it. Please do not send passwords, one-time SMS verification codes, or WhatsApp linking secrets. We will contact you as needed to verify ownership before acting.

Eligible deletion requests may cover user account identifiers, saved farm and field information, crop and harvest records, uploaded media, and related WhatsApp linking records. We will review the request, explain what can be deleted and what must be retained for security or legal reasons, and confirm the outcome. Backup copies and historical operational records may require additional time or separate handling.

Stop WhatsApp messages without deleting farm data

Open Farm Platform's WhatsApp linking screen and select Disconnect WhatsApp, or send STOP from your linked WhatsApp number. This withdraws channel consent; it is not a full account deletion request.
