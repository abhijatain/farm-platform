Farm Platform, operated by [OPERATOR NAME], provides farm recordkeeping, maps, agricultural imagery analysis and an optional WhatsApp interface. Contact: [PUBLIC EMAIL].

Information collected

We process phone numbers and Firebase authentication IDs; optional profile names and languages; farm names, field boundaries and coordinates; crop, season, activity, harvest and growth-stage records; uploaded photos, videos and captions; and processed satellite metrics. Technical requests and error information may be collected for operating and securing the service.

WhatsApp information

If you opt into WhatsApp linking, the service processes your WhatsApp account identifier, linked number, language preference, consent and linking status, incoming message identifiers and type metadata, and outgoing menu and delivery status data. Incoming commands are inspected to provide service and are not saved as chat bodies in the application's database. Linking codes are stored as hashes. This channel is optional and currently supports read-only farm menus.

Why we process information

We use information to authenticate users, store and display farm records, deliver mapping and weather context, process satellite observations and media, send requested WhatsApp service replies and diagnose technical or security issues. We do not use the current WhatsApp service for marketing.

Third-party services

Relevant providers include Google Firebase for phone verification, Google Cloud Storage for uploaded media and generated data, Meta for WhatsApp messages, MapTiler for map services, and weather and satellite data providers. Information necessary to provide requested functions, such as mapped field locations for weather requests, may be transmitted to these services. Their own terms and notices may apply. We do not sell farmer account data or farm records.

Security and retention

Farm records are stored in a database, while uploaded media and some derived imagery are stored in private cloud storage. Authenticated endpoints restrict access to owned farm records. Data may remain while an account is active or where needed for service integrity, security and legal obligations. WhatsApp audit and delivery metadata may remain after channel disconnection; no fixed deletion schedule is promised in the present development version.

Your choices and deletion requests

You can disconnect WhatsApp in the application or send STOP from your linked WhatsApp number to withdraw service-message consent. This does not automatically delete your farm account. For access, correction or deletion requests, email [PUBLIC EMAIL] with subject Farm Platform Privacy Request. We may verify ownership before fulfilling a request, and explain any retention required by law or for security. See our data deletion instructions.

Other information

Farm Platform is intended for adults. Providers may process information in other countries. We may update this notice and its last-updated date as the service changes.
